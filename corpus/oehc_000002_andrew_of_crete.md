Month of July IV.

The life of our holy father Andrew of Jerusalem, who became Archbishop of Crete, writer to Nikitas the most famous patrician and cystor.

Lord, bless.

1. It is not lawful, nor holy, for the righteous and holy men on earth to be baptized into the depths of oblivion, nor to be baptized into the depths of oblivion for the sake of purity, or to be revealed in the manner of silence for a long time , but on the lampstand of the narration openly, so that from the good deeds revealed, the sanctuary to the good God may re-establish glory and profit of great benefit to men
Treasure [p.170] To the most important of these he should imitate the most righteous things.They do not rightly listen to this present word as grateful, that we also may suffer from it, those who have no praise, that it does not worthily praise the worthy, the light that is from the light, Christ, invoking extensively, the co-ruler of the Father, and the Eternal of the Holy Spirit, the One of the One Thrice-Holy Godhead, Who fills all things with righteousness, Who bestows gifts of wisdom, and satisfies all knowledge, And having been filled with him the great gifts of his saying, let us begin a narrative of the venerable father Andrew, the Archbishop of Crete, a virtuous and noble island of Christ.

2. Did he become a child of God-loving and pious parents, George and Gregory, whom the state helped to name?For both of them, having diligently watched in good works, they saw George of God, and brought forth righteousness to him.And Damascus was not a city to them, nor a city of the West, as one of the enemies of the unbelievers, but one of the East, which had not overshadowed them in the east of the Orthodox faith.In which also the offspring of these, but rather the servant of God, came out of their loins?For it is necessary to sprout a good branch from the root of a good one, and to sprout a branch.And when he is silent in his tongue from birth, and until seven years of age, he is insulted by God, so that if he have the temperance of his tongue from infancy, having learned it in infancy, he will secretly kiss God's mouth like Moses, and take away a yoke from his youth.And it is good for the plow to attack himself, and he does not turn backwards, but misunderstands them to those who are ahead, so that if he should present to God the news of his works, and the agriculture of a hundred and a hundred people.And eight years after this event, in one of the days, as it was with him with the
[p.171] His parents, and it was his former custom in the holy Church of God, and he ceased to perform the divine liturgy after the partaking of the precious and life-giving body and blood of the flesh By the providence of our great God and Saviour Jesus Christ, let him leave the silence of the seven days of the present as the seven years of the presentHis life being patterned by good conduct in quietude;And his voice in the eighth time of the eighth and first unfading day of the infinite life to come, declares that through his good works with God he will always speak to God [lege: it will be] to
speak.

3. Immediately then, having been duly taught the prose letters, he is very eminent in prudence, and learns the higher.Hast thou collected the best grammar for the most wise chastisement of thyself, as a bee that is competent;Flowers are conveniently chosen for work, and he is brought by his God-loving parents, who is called by God to all godliness, this pious youth, according to his second To the Most Holy Church of the Holy Resurrection of Christ in Jerusalem, that the death of the passions, as the life-giving God who is to live, may be cared for.And by Theodore, who was then the Patriarch of those who were alive, and they were in holy remembrance, receiving and being deposed, and having been disinherited in the sanctuary. By the shedding of the hairs, the pleasures of life were cast away as fluid, and to the sacred crown of his head he crowned the soul with the virtues according to God.And he was in the patriarchate with the divine scriptures of perseverance and the benefit of the soul reaped from them, progressing in grace and ascents of good things in his heart.And God was with him, his own servant, free from all evil deeds, and because of the glorified gloom and the greatest reverence of
his pious [p.172] Of this Patriarchate of Notaries is honored in order.And the aforesaid Patriarch of Jerusalem, marveling at his soberness in all things, and with all leniency and grace, he called Theodore his most graceful to the end of his life, But he duly commanded him to do the ecclesiastical thing after his death, without the opinion and counsel of this pious and God-loving man, that he should not be governed, but that he should do everything with his consent.For to the mystical eyes, as a spiritual man, the deeds of this spiritual man were manifestly seen, and he was young in old age, and was a champion of ecclesiastical affairs, raising up the trophies with the weapons of godliness and good administration.And the man who was in the habit of the house became the perceiver and the living creature, and in the righteousness of things he would command him as a guide.And he was a monk, who was a servant with God and God, many times in good things, showing all holiness to the priests, and to the poor richly O father of orphans, an avenger of affliction, of sorrowful joy, of those who do not remember the dawn of consolation, and to all things apostolically for God's sake, he is admired by all in all.

4. And of the Holy and Ecumenical Sixth Synod in the Theophylactic and King City, as the head of the body of every most Christian regime, pious applauded during the reign of Constantine, who was then the living Orthodox of ChristFather Justinian, and the weeds of cacodoxy in the sickle of the pious from the roots, and the two natures and wills and energies of the one Holy and Life-giving Trinity
[p.173] Our Lord Jesus Christ, having well ordained, and for the healing of those who are sick with these things in a God-loving way, as everywhere in the most holy churches of God, which are from the Eu. By the said Holy Sixth Synod, which was duly instituted, and according to the Church of Jerusalem in writing by the king, and all that were gathered there for this purpose, both priests and God-loving men, And with great joy they embraced the writings of the Orthodox dogmas, and quenched every flame of cacodox thirst, as if from the source of Orthodoxy their waters were satiated, the man before us, the worthy man He was a servant of God, even at a young age, having chosen great for the modesty of his manners, and having handled and trusted to him the copies of the pious dogmas which had been found, That is the confession of their true faith, and with two pious elders, they sent him back to the same most pious king.And the king himself, who had been transferred to the best life, and had left this perishable life, to succeed the scepter of the kingdom to his son Justinian, who had also received copies of the words of piety by this pious man, and a man who was consecrated to God.And when he had received from the Orthodox king Justinian, who had received the pious things concerning his acceptance, and having entrusted them to those who were with him, he sent them to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, from whence he was sent back.

5. Therefore he who walketh in every way of salvation abides within the bounds of good?For what is better than the servant of God and Master for his own servant, which is foreseen on a pretext of convenience, the transfer from the land of the enemies, as from Jericho to Jerusalem, the queen of the cities is given to him in his own interest.
[p. 174] In which he sits and has the power of grace, apostolically pleasing, he entertains himself from on high, as a disciple of the disciples and teachers of the whole world, and an imitator of their deeds and their divine teaching.For by the seduction of his words for correction, he hath spiritually awakened many men, and has brought to God a reasonable ophson.For in silence for most of the time when he was in peace, and in his own cell, God was not pleased to conceal him for a long time, but for the benefit of his soul and of his neighbor He revealed it, that he might benefit from others, and for the benefit of others.The things of his good conduct are thus made known to the king at that time, according to the oikonomia of the king above, who reigns over the kings on earth, and he is appointed a deacon, according to the royal command, In the great catholic church of God, great in the priestly order of the deacons, so that even the presbyters admire the mind of an elder in his youth;And after a while the great one in DidasA good father becomes the father of orphans, the entrusted care of the well-to-do orphanage, and as a noble to the virtues according to God, and a rich lover of the poor, and the administration of the pious ministry of the Eugenios.And the strait of these two places, the mystic of the Trinity, to the wideness of many good things, immediately conveyed, and with a good and good administration he duly raised the lower texts, to the one good place, Thou hast orphaned orphan in the righteous and paternal conception, and hast rejected the strain of the other benevolent house in the breadth, and fed the poor by unceasing food, like another wise Joseph, not in Egypt, but in the houses of God to those who are poor as brethren.From thence, therefore, as from power to power, and from glory to glory, as from God
[p.175] Being called, but he resigned for humility, the poor in spirit for the kingdom of heaven, and not wanting to enter the higher, having been duly voted by worthy as a worthy archbishop of the Cretan, a Christ-loving island.

6. And he was restored to the throne prepared for him by God of the archbishopric, having received from him every priestly order and of the Christ-loving people with great joy as a shepherd of godliness, If he had entrusted to him rational sheep, as in the pen of the church of God, he had secured himself with a rod of power to the weapon of the life-giving passion, on the cross, skillfully to the noble and intelligible ones enjoying the wild animals, And with the sling of unceasing prayers, severely wounding them for the greatest authority of the priesthood that had attacked him, as if he knew the impending burden on those who entrusted to the reason of God.And he addressed a word unto the people, by him manifestly showing what was said to the priests to have a burden.For the virtue of the former hesychia persuaded him to feel sorry for him, and to suffer the damage, as if he meant his treasure, to destroy;And he who gives a word with the opening of his mouth, the word of God, and through the prophet David, exclaimed, "Enlarge thy mouth, and I will fill it," he, as a forewarner of the wisdom of the heart by meditating on the wisdom of the heart of this noble man, rather having filled his mouth with prudence, has enlarged his majesty to the glory.Wherefore also words of praise are different from the All-Immaculate and Superior of all creation, the Virgin by nature and above nature, the Mother of the Son of God and the true God, with the Father. He expressed joy and rejoicing, and then to the abruptness for the truth of John the Baptist and other holy and venerable fathers.

[p. 176] 

7. Wherefore, therefore, he has made himself the temple of God out of good works, according to Paul, the holy apostle and disciple of the Lord and the teacher of the end, the temple of God that is worthy is the temple of God The temple of the most immaculate and all-praised Theotokos Mary, the temple animated and sanctified by the Word of God, Blacherna, such a temple not built by him, was built from a new templeThou hast named, priests ministers of the monastic form, in it for the hymns and glorification of God, in a godly manner, as an antidorus of the great gifts and understandings which came from him from the gate of God?And he set good things on good things every day, for it was not a day for him that the light of righteousness did not rise from deeds, as the neighbor of benefactors, and drew near to God on the other side, to those who are bodily [lege: bodily?]As well as being sick out of poverty, this old man, and in old age, set up a house of consolation and hospitality, which is called a Guest House from the new, and in good work he set up every self-sufficient need for Christ out of necessity. As the things of God God offering with joy, and as the word of wisdom, this most wise man has learned and known, that "sins are cleansed by alms and faith; "And hath he made a ministry as a poor in spirit to the poor ministers, and poureth water into the washbasin, which he shall bring self-sufficiency out of his own money, as from vessels in the place of need.And having girded the right as a Lentian faith, he had not only his feet, but also washed away his whole soul, and departed.Being therefore a faithful priest of the great priest, and having perished through the heavens, as in the altar of his own soul, he received God every day of sacrifice, and the neglected ones he diligently rebuilt ecclesiastical temples, and all these things for
him [p.[177] A world of sacred relics was duly provided by this universal man, and he decently adorned them.

8. And what has God done to his enemies for his own servant and friend?For the Hagarenes, having set their course in the sea, by a multitude of boats on the Christ-loving island of Cretans, they stood up with great enthusiasm, so that the judgment of God might condemn them as condemners for their unjust enmarches against the Christians, even in Crete.For in the stronghold of the Fierce One, in which the man of God with the most Christian people shepherded by him for the hostile The barbarians sat down to the siege, and many machines to line up wars in it, and did they deprive themselves of their inaction, their own destruction for profit.They were not subject to captivity, having been fought by the arrows of the prayers of God as a bow to the eye of a priest.

9. Such a man of Himself hath been made glorious, and a pestilential disease hath fallen upon the people of the island according to him, and has fallen into drought according to the chastisement of God, the one who has fallen from God He was despised to be sent to the thirsty land unceasingly and insatiably, as if he thirst for the righteousness of God<or>syne, offering him a shower of tears, and bringing from him the things of his ears, And as he had been anointed with many fasts, and unceasing vigils, and unceasing prayers, he offered sickness to the Lord, and received the healing from Christ the Saviour, who was wounded and healed for us, and Our redeeming God, and all were all things unto God, interceding through supplications and safeguarding the harmless, admonishing all, and guiding them to abstain from evil deeds and to the good [p.178] To make the kingdom of the divine commandments walk on a path, which leads to the kingdom of heaven.And it was necessary to summon him in such a way, that he might ascend to the God-guarded city and the kingdom of cities, and many were laboriously brought to him for the sake of benefit, and the [lege: the] When they had seen him, and had turned away the angry and wrathful from themselves, they were in peace, and others had brought forth righteousness to God in the year of his teaching.And when they were enlisted to him, and having been well taught by him, they were content with their cowards, and not to divulge anyone, and willingly broke down the iniquitous thing on this side.And the poor of him, being richly nourished by him, offered thanksgiving to God.

10. Then then is the end of his temporal life, as to the eternal life called by God, and the resurrection from thence is mystically manifested to him from above, and he foretells it to his friends and virtuous men, that as he is still alive in his metropolis, he shall not be returned?And with joy he embraced them all, and was broken by a holy kiss, and released them from Constantinople, and having taken the island of Mitylene, which was thus called, he took possession of the island, not as it happened, but according to providence, in a place called Erissos, as he had ceased there.Wherefore, therefore, thou hast kept the faith well, and hast duly carried out the spiritual way, in peace, and hast fallen asleep there, placing the soul by the hand of the living God, and making the offerings unto Him, for us.And the bed of his rest took place in the church of the holy martyr Anastasia, in which he also receives the sound of the dead with a trumpet
[p.179] Resurrection on the undying day of the second day, with much glory upon the surface of "the coming judgment of the living and the dead of our Lord Jesus Christ," from whence shall he also receive the reward of the pains of his works, as he had endured the whole day in the heat?And his dormition in Christ took place on the fourth of July, the eighth month of the judgment.And to God and the Father, together with the Son and God, with the all-holy and consubstantial Spirit, the one triune divinity, Who shone forth glory, honor, and worship from all of us as a light of righteousness in the past, glory, honor, and worship by all of us is due for the ages of ages.Amen.
